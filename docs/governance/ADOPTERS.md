# Production Adopters

This registry tracks verified **Unaffiliated Production Adopters** under
`docs/governance/COVENANT.md` section 6 (foundation transfer Trigger (a)).

## Qualification Criteria

An organisation counts toward Trigger (a) when:
1. It has no ownership, employment, contractor, or funding affiliation with Loonaro.
2. It runs Guardrails ADR in production on at least **25 persistent endpoints** OR at least
   **1 multi-endpoint server deployment** for at least **90 consecutive days**.
3. An authorised representative submits a PR appending a factual entry below.
