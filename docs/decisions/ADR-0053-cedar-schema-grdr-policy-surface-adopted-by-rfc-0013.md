## Context
WI-0018 (`#21`) introduces the `Grdr` v1alpha1 Cedar schema, strict request builders and the verdict derivation and degradation semantics across `grdr-schema`, `grdr-model`, `grdr-core` and `grdr-policy-ref`. Under D-61, [`docs/design/03`](../design/03-system-architecture-and-modularity.md) section 7 and [`docs/design/30`](../design/30-open-source-governance-and-community.md) section 3, neither a frozen Cedar schema, nor a new public crate surface, nor new third-party dependencies (`cedar-policy`, `proptest`), nor an amendment to RFC 0007 or RFC 0009 may land without an accepted RFC.

Planning the slice-0 sub-issues of `#21` also raised three design conflicts that downstream issues could not settle on their own ([RFC 0013, Motivation](../../rfcs/0013-cedar-schema-grdr-policy-surface.md#motivation); `lead-core` planning note `https://github.com/416rehman/Guardrails/issues/431#issuecomment-6859820526`):
- [`docs/design/12`](../design/12-policy-engine.md) section 2 stated both that context records are always present with neutral values and that an unresolved command marks `context.command.classes` absent, while worked policy 5 (`destructive.infra-teardown`) and ten sibling rules in section 5 read `context.*` attributes without `has` guards (and all 22 worked rules across sections 3 and 5 rely on `[Session, Process]` or `[Session]` principal scopes).
- `docs/design/12` section 2 named `grdr-schema/policy/v1alpha1.cedarschema`, whereas WI-0018 (`#21`) and `#435` placed it at `schema/cedar/` alongside `schema/codes/`.
- `#434` forbids `grdr-policy-ref` from taking any normal dependency on `grdr-core` or `cedar-policy` (`cargo tree -p grdr-policy-ref -e normal` clean), while `#1595` implements `derive` and `degrade` inside `grdr_core::policy` and `#1597` tests `grdr_policy_ref::{derive, degrade}` over the exact same Rust types with zero shared evaluator logic (`D-63`).

## Decision
**Accepted.** The Cedar schema `Grdr` v1alpha1, `grdr_schema::cedar`, `grdr_core::policy`, the shared pure policy contract in `grdr-model`, and the `cedar-policy` and `proptest` admissions are those RFC 0013 states at pre-acceptance reviewed blob `7483e5ce7fe23c96f0ebda5a053af439e3cd05ce` (SHA-256 `3a36859f1eb5ae2b963c673626309ebfa31ce7ca55d636cf870634fbe1a4edfb`), written against spine pin `a80859235e7a2a4444648d6fa8d389d78dde6be9` (SHA-256 `8f6ea876777c0121ecec068c0bc4cef45932be45c1c2aff4140712db1e06628d`) ([RFC 0013, Design](../../rfcs/0013-cedar-schema-grdr-policy-surface.md#design)). In outline:

1. **Cedar schema `Grdr` v1alpha1 (R1).**
   - Fixes the verbatim `.cedarschema` text for Cedar 4.14.1 (language 4.6), declaring 18 entity types: 6 principal-side-only (`Organisation`, `Group`, `Device`, `AgentInstance`, `Session`, `Process`) and 12 resource types each carrying `classes: Set<String>` (`Agent` shared with `AgentInstance` and `agent.spawn`, `Executable`, `Path`, `Endpoint`, `ModelEndpoint`, `McpServer`, `McpTool`, `Credential`, `ConfigTarget`, `ElevationRoute`, `UiTarget`, `Sink`), with zero human-identifying entity attributes (`P8`, `D-51`).
   - Declares 4 action groups without `appliesTo` (`any`, `file`, `net`, `mcp`) and 15 leaf actions matching the spine action classes verbatim: 13 operational actions (`exec`, `file.read`, `file.write`, `file.delete`, `net.connect`, `net.request`, `llm.request`, `mcp.connect`, `mcp.call`, `cred.use`, `config.change`, `elevate`, `ui.act`) admit `[Session, Process]`, while `agent.spawn` and `data.flow` admit `[Session]` only.
   - Declares `CommonContext` (including reserved empty required `ScheduleContext = {}`) and per-action context records, using Cedar `String` attributes emitted solely from closed Rust enums for closed vocabularies, and validates all 22 worked rules of `docs/design/12` sections 3 and 5 strictly with zero rule edits.

2. **Schema path, `grdr-schema::cedar` embedding and RFC 0007 amendment (R2).**
   - Places the canonical schema at `schema/cedar/v1alpha1.cedarschema` and embeds it in layer-0 `crates/grdr-schema/src/cedar.rs` as `SCHEMA_VERSION = "v1alpha1"` and `V1ALPHA1 = include_str!("../../../schema/cedar/v1alpha1.cedarschema")` with zero new dependencies, `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, and `#![deny(clippy::arithmetic_side_effects)]`.
   - Amends [RFC 0007](../../rfcs/0007-grdr-model-and-grdr-schema-slice-0-surface.md) sections 2 R10, 8 and 11 ([ADR-0038](ADR-0038-the-grdr-model-and-grdr-schema-slice-0-surface-adopted-by-rfc-0007.md)) without editing `rfcs/0007-grdr-model-and-grdr-schema-slice-0-surface.md`: adds `crates/grdr-schema/src/cedar.rs` as an eighth source file, updates `tests/construction.rs` `layout` incrementally over declared modules in `{codes, limits, health, cedar}` (matching RFC 0016), allows `include_str!` and the `rustc --emit=dep-info` edge on `schema/cedar/v1alpha1.cedarschema` solely for `crates/grdr-schema/src/cedar.rs`, and tracks `crates/grdr-schema/src/cedar.rs` in [`security/trust-core.paths`](../../security/trust-core.paths) (`#2075`).

3. **Required context fields, neutral values and engine-side `absent`/`pending` (R3).**
   - Keeps every Cedar context record and field required in `v1alpha1.cedarschema`, populated with typed neutral values (`[]`, `false`, `0`, `""`, `{}`) when missing or unresolved (`attribution.grade = 0`, `verified = false`, empty `session.labels` for `Process`).
   - Keeps `absent` and `pending` as engine-side `BTreeSet<ContextField>` sets on `CedarRequest` rather than Cedar `Context` attributes, preventing policy rules from branching on `absent` or `pending` directly and preserving Rows 6 and 7 semantics.

4. **`grdr_core::policy` surface, `grdr-model` shared contract and RFC 0009 amendment (R4).**
   - Keeps `grdr-core` at `class = "experimental"` in [`surfaces.toml`](../../surfaces.toml) with its three-key `[[crate]]` schema `(name, class, rfc)` unchanged, tracking `ARCH-007` public exposure of `cedar_policy::{Schema, Request, Entities}` via [`architecture.toml`](../../architecture.toml) (`#2075`) and requiring opaque Guardrails wrapper types before `beta`/`stable` graduation (`D-60`).
   - Amends [RFC 0009](../../rfcs/0009-grdr-model-slice-0-surface.md) sections 8, 9 and 13 ([ADR-0041](ADR-0041-the-grdr-model-slice-0-surface-adopted-by-rfc-0009.md)) without editing `rfcs/0009-grdr-model-slice-0-surface.md`: hosts the shared pure contract `wire_enum!` enums (`ActionClass`, `ControlPoint`, `Verdict`, `ExecutedVerb`, `DerivationRow`, `ModifyBudget`, `EnforcedMode`, `ApprovalGrade`, `Obligation`, `Transform`, `NormaliserOutcome`, `ControlPointCoverage`, `DecisionReason`), `PendingWindow`, `PolicyRuleId`, `#[non_exhaustive] PolicyRuleIdError` (`Coded` `GRDR-MODEL-0012` + `LogSafe` via `LogValue::Code`, no `Display`/`Error`), `ForbidMatch`, `NotEvaluableRule`, `ControlPointCaps`, `DeriveInput`, `Derived` and `Executed` at `grdr-model`'s crate root (reusing `grdr_model::Tamper` and `BTreeSet` determinism) and re-exports them from `grdr_core::policy::{self, derive, degrade}`, so `grdr-policy-ref` links only `grdr-model` (`D-63`).
   - Defines `grdr_core::policy::{schema, request, derive, degrade}` in layer 1 (no I/O, no clock, no entropy, no `unsafe`, no panic paths, `#![deny(clippy::arithmetic_side_effects)]`, `wasm32-unknown-unknown`, `ARCH-013` isolation from `analyze`/`session`/`detect`/`discover`/`compile`), typed request inputs (`OpaqueId` with byte-length redaction in `Debug`, `ClassTag`, `ContextField`, `PrincipalInput`, `CommonInput`, `ExecInput` plus 14 action inputs), `#[non_exhaustive] PolicyError` (`LogSafe` via `Code`), strict derivation order (Rows 1 to 9, `ModifyBudget`, rule-local `user_local_time_lock`, Row 7 `os_control_point_attached` vs `fail_closed` vs coverage-gap branching, `Snapshot` must-obligation) and total `degrade` using `u128` widened arithmetic over `POLICY_HOLD_DEADLINE_SHARE` and `POLICY_FILE_HOLD_CAP`.

5. **Dependency admissions (R5).**
   - Admits `cedar-policy = "=4.14.1"` (`default-features = false`, `Apache-2.0`) as a normal dependency of `grdr-core` only (`[arch004] content_parsers` isolation in `architecture.toml`) and `proptest` (`MIT OR Apache-2.0`, `1.12.x`) as a dev-dependency of `grdr-core` (`#432`) and `grdr-policy-ref` (`#434`), subject to [`supply-chain/licences.toml`](../../supply-chain/licences.toml), `cargo deny check` and `cargo vet`.

6. **`POLICY` error codes and `limits.toml` bounds (R6).**
   - Registers `[area.POLICY]` (`GRDR-POLICY-0001` to `0012`) and `GRDR-MODEL-0012` (`RULE_ID_FORM`) in `schema/codes/` via `#278` (with source-locale tokens routed under [RFC 0010](../../rfcs/0010-code-tokens-before-the-token-check.md) / [ADR-0044](ADR-0044-code-tokens-before-the-token-check-adopted-by-rfc-0010.md) via `#1853`).
   - Registers the twelve `[bound.policy.*]` bounds (including `policy.parser-input-max` at `bytes = 262144` mitigating the 2026 Cedar parser memory advisory noted in `docs/design/03` section 13, `policy.rule-id-max`, and `policy.class-tag-max`) in `limits.toml` via `#279` under RFC 0007 section 6 naming rules.

7. **No pin and no evidence.**
   - RFC 0013 decides no pinned path of [`rfcs/pins.toml`](../../rfcs/pins.toml), which stays unchanged at `docs/design/00-decisions.md` blob `a80859235e7a2a4444648d6fa8d389d78dde6be9` (`sha256` `8f6ea876777c0121ecec068c0bc4cef45932be45c1c2aff4140712db1e06628d`). No file is added under `rfcs/evidence/`, and [`rfcs/0007-grdr-model-and-grdr-schema-slice-0-surface.md`](../../rfcs/0007-grdr-model-and-grdr-schema-slice-0-surface.md) and [`rfcs/0009-grdr-model-slice-0-surface.md`](../../rfcs/0009-grdr-model-slice-0-surface.md) are not edited ([RFC 0001](../../rfcs/0001-adopt-the-spine.md#design); [ADR-0036](ADR-0036-the-amended-spine-is-the-baseline-adopted-by-rfc-0001.md)).

8. **What this acceptance binds.**
   - Changing `schema/cedar/v1alpha1.cedarschema`, adding, removing or modifying public items, enum variants, signatures or semantics of `grdr_core::policy` or the shared policy contract in `grdr-model`, or promoting `grdr-core` out of `experimental` requires a new amending RFC naming RFC 0013 (and RFC 0009 when touching `grdr-model`, or RFC 0007 when touching `grdr-schema` layout/constraining rules). Adding further codes in `schema/codes/policy.toml` or `schema/codes/model.toml` follows RFC 0007 section 4 ("Content, not surface") and RFC 0010 without amending RFC 0013; changing `[bound.policy.*]` values follows RFC 0007 section 6's trust-core `limits.toml` lane.

## Consequences
- Unblocks `#432`, `#434`, `#435`, `#436`, `#1600`, `#1595`, `#1597`, `#278`, `#279`, `#2075` and `#2076` from `#431` once `#431`'s acceptance PR merges to `main`, giving `WI-0018` one frozen Cedar schema, one request/derivation/degradation surface and resolved error/bound registries.
- All 22 worked policies of `docs/design/12` sections 3 and 5 validate strictly with zero rule edits, while policy authors cannot read or branch on `absent` or `pending` directly in Cedar.
- `grdr-policy-ref` and `grdr_core::policy` share exact pure Rust types hosted in `grdr-model` with zero shared evaluator code and zero `cedar-policy` dependency in `grdr-policy-ref` (`D-63`), requiring `lead-core` to coordinate `#1595`/`#1597` write sets with `crates/grdr-model/**`.
- Exposing `cedar_policy::{Schema, Request, Entities}` directly in `grdr_core::policy` stays scoped to `grdr-core` `class = "experimental"` (`ARCH-007`) and requires wrapping behind opaque Guardrails types before `grdr-core` can graduate to `beta` or `stable` (`D-60`).
- Adding fields to `ScheduleContext` later requires a schema version bump (`v1alpha2`) rather than adding optional attributes to `v1alpha1`.

## Alternatives rejected
- Those of [RFC 0013, Alternatives](../../rfcs/0013-cedar-schema-grdr-policy-surface.md#alternatives).
- Adding a pin record or `rfcs/evidence/` files: RFC 0013 decides no pinned path, so either is out of route ([RFC 0001](../../rfcs/0001-adopt-the-spine.md#design); [ADR-0036](ADR-0036-the-amended-spine-is-the-baseline-adopted-by-rfc-0001.md)).
- Editing `rfcs/0007-grdr-model-and-grdr-schema-slice-0-surface.md` or `rfcs/0009-grdr-model-slice-0-surface.md` in place, or modifying RFC 0013's reviewed `Summary`, `Motivation`, `Design`, `Surfaces changed`, `Alternatives`, `Drawbacks` or `Unresolved questions` text: RFC 0001 ("RFC states", "Review binds the decision") prohibits editing accepted RFCs or changing reviewed body prose after review.
- Adding extra keys to `surfaces.toml` `[[crate]]` entries to record `cedar-policy` exposure: violates RFC 0005 R6.1's fixed `(name, class, rfc)` schema; `ARCH-007` tracking belongs in `architecture.toml` (`#2075`) and this decision record.

## Revisit when
- A later comment or implementation item uncovers a defect in `schema/cedar/v1alpha1.cedarschema`, `grdr_core::policy` or the shared `grdr-model` policy contract (`docs/design/30` section 3), opening a new amending RFC naming RFC 0013 (and RFC 0007 or RFC 0009 where touched).
- `#739` (`RFC-1`) supersedes or extends `grdr-model` policy type conventions while keeping `v1alpha1.cedarschema` and `grdr_core::policy` semantics intact.
- `ScheduleContext` gains time-window attributes (`v1alpha2`), or `grdr-core` prepares to graduate out of `experimental` in `surfaces.toml` (requiring opaque wrappers around `cedar_policy::{Schema, Request, Entities}` under `ARCH-007` and `D-60`).
- A RustSec advisory or breaking change affects `cedar-policy = "=4.14.1"` (`#432` / `design/12` section 2 trust-core upgrade route).
