## Context
[`docs/design/11-endpoint-engineering.md`](../design/11-endpoint-engineering.md) section 11 defines daemon health as a condition set mapped to the surface states `Protected`, `Degraded` and `Safe mode`, plus the independent `Stale` flag, and refers to `grdr-schema/health.toml` and `health.<code>.*` language tokens (`D-69`). Before RFC 0016:

- No RFC specified the TOML format, validation refusals, never-change `history` check, or generated layer-0 Rust types (`grdr_schema::health`), preventing `#147` (`HealthSet`) from making unregistered health codes a compile error (`HealthCode` sealed) and reading fixed `severity`, allowed `scopes`, `surface` and `stale` flags solely from registry data.
- First-run path items (`#149`, `#421`, `#427`, `#1538`, `#1673`), doc generation (`#1636`), runbook generation (`#1644`), and freshness checks (`#1629` `F2`/`F7`) lacked frozen slice-0 condition codes and token paths, including `#1673`'s three key-custody and fail-safe publish conditions (`R3` and `R6`).
- `design/11` named `grdr-schema/health.toml` and a fresh RFC per code, conflicting with [RFC 0007](../../rfcs/0007-grdr-model-and-grdr-schema-slice-0-surface.md)'s `schema/` layout and `"Content, not surface"` rule, while `#675` (`EN-DOC04`) and `#1687` (`R3`) moved `health.sensor` onto the `decision, A` lane emitted on change only alongside periodic `Heartbeat` (`design/22`) and `bulk` roll-ups.
- `grdr-schema` is a layer-0 public crate (`surface = "public"`), so its public surface changes only by RFC (`D-61`, [ADR-0001](ADR-0001-decisions-are-adrs-and-public-surfaces-change-by-rfc.md)).

RFC 0016 landed on `main` in `State: Review` via PR `#2041` (draft commit `b3bdad1e23c7c594468ed231e84aaedfe967fc93`, Round 2 revision `29b5d444a953160440c9ef12d6f2bb0aa88405d1`, squash-merged at `1771fa281fb1c55c51b961070e089e58095e5a88`; reviewed RFC file blob `90aec1dd91c95a528302ebde29cfd1e77b62e48b`, SHA-256 `76e4364b46856def1fcd7244aaaa9730a815721dd70ac38a9852898bda89e7e0`), written against the spine pin in force `a80859235e7a2a4444648d6fa8d389d78dde6be9` (SHA-256 `8f6ea876777c0121ecec068c0bc4cef45932be45c1c2aff4140712db1e06628d`).

Following [ADR-0043](ADR-0043-lean-token-optimised-agent-team.md) (`#2005`, [`.claude/risk-tiers.toml`](../../.claude/risk-tiers.toml) `[checklist_addons].rfc`), PR `#2041` completed Round 2 review on blob `90aec1dd91c95a528302ebde29cfd1e77b62e48b` (`Proposed text: none`, base `a80859235e7a2a4444648d6fa8d389d78dde6be9`) without a separate 9-seat persona council fan-out, and both verbatim passing verdicts are recorded on `#668`:
- `eval-review` (`Google`, `Gemini 4 Carbon`, Round 2, `pass`): https://github.com/416rehman/Guardrails/issues/668#issuecomment-7099935189
- `eval-security` (`Google`, `Gemini 4 Neon`, Round 2, `pass`): https://github.com/416rehman/Guardrails/issues/668#issuecomment-7099935784
- CI checks on `29b5d444a953160440c9ef12d6f2bb0aa88405d1` and merge commit `1771fa281fb1c55c51b961070e089e58095e5a88`: `verify`, `docs`, `scan`, `codeql`, and `fuzz` passed (`ClusterFuzzLite` skipped until fuzz targets exist).

## Decision
**Accepted.** RFC 0016 is accepted on reviewed RFC file blob `90aec1dd91c95a528302ebde29cfd1e77b62e48b` (SHA-256 `76e4364b46856def1fcd7244aaaa9730a815721dd70ac38a9852898bda89e7e0`) against spine pin `a80859235e7a2a4444648d6fa8d389d78dde6be9` ([RFC 0016, Design](../../rfcs/0016-health-condition-registry.md#design)). In outline:

1. **Boundary with `#675` (`EN-DOC04`) and telemetry lanes (section 1).** `schema/health.toml` (`grdr_schema::health`) holds runtime daemon condition codes (`HealthSet`, `status.pb`, `grdr doctor`, control-plane `Heartbeat`, `/codes/<code>`, `docs/runbooks/`) and never registers telemetry event class names (`health.sensor`, `health.gap`, `health.coverage`). `health.sensor` resides in [`docs/design/04-event-model-and-schemas.md`](../design/04-event-model-and-schemas.md) section 4 (`decision, A`) and is emitted **on change only** (never timer-driven); periodic condition summaries and loss counters travel via control-plane `Heartbeat` ([`docs/design/22-transport-fleet-and-updates.md`](../design/22-transport-fleet-and-updates.md)) and `bulk` roll-ups.
2. **Locations and RFC 0007 amendments (section 2).** The registry lives at `schema/health.toml` (owned by `squad:core` in [`.claude/owners.toml`](../../.claude/owners.toml)) and generates `crates/grdr-schema/src/health/generated.rs` via `crates/grdr-schema/tests/gen/health.rs` (`tests/health_gen.rs`, `[[test]]` with `autotests = false`), with source tokens in `interface/strings/src/health.mf2.toml`. Three amendments apply to [RFC 0007](../../rfcs/0007-grdr-model-and-grdr-schema-slice-0-surface.md):
   - **Section 2 R10 (`layout`):** `crates/grdr-schema/src/` holds ten files across `lib.rs`, `codes/*`, `limits/*`, and `health/*`; `tests/construction.rs` `layout` accepts `lib.rs` plus the exact 3-file triplet (`<m>.rs`, `<m>/registry.rs`, `<m>/generated.rs`) for each registered module (`codes`, `limits`, `health`) whose `generated.rs` exists in the checkout.
   - **Section 8 (`security/trust-core.paths`):** `#2058` adds `crates/grdr-schema/src/health.rs`, `crates/grdr-schema/src/health/registry.rs`, `crates/grdr-schema/tests/health_gen.rs`, and `crates/grdr-schema/tests/fixtures/health/**` (keeping `src/health/generated.rs` off the list).
   - **CI `drift` and `history`:** `tests/health_gen.rs` `drift()` integrates with `#1943` R2's `<module>_gen` loop, and `#2058` adds `.github/workflows/schema-health-history.yml` running base's `history()` against head's `schema/health.toml` as bounded regular-file data (`#1943` R5).
3. **Layer-0 purity and `grdr_schema::health` surface (sections 3 and 4).** Zero normal `[dependencies]`, `#![no_std]` compatible, no `alloc`, no I/O, no `Display`, checked/saturating arithmetic, no panic paths, `wasm32-unknown-unknown` ready. `generated::Seal(())` keeps `HealthCode`, `HealthEntry`, `Retirement` and `ConditionState::Retired` sealed to `health::generated`. Active codes expose `pub const` `HealthCode` bindings (`ACTIVE`), while `ALL` and `find(&str) -> Option<&'static HealthEntry>` cover both active and retired entries without allowing callers to turn a string or retired entry into a `HealthCode`. Derived strings are `health.<code>.{meaning,cause,fix}` and `codes/<code>`; `scopes` emit in canonical `HealthScope` order.
4. **Registry grammar, refusals, and never-change `history` rules (section 5).** Each `[[condition]]` validates `code` (`^[a-z][a-z0-9]*(?:-[a-z0-9]+)*$`, 1 to 63 bytes, unique `SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE`), `severity` (`info | warn | degraded | critical`), non-empty duplicate-free `scopes`, `surface` (`none | safe-mode`, where `safe-mode` requires `critical`), `stale`, `status` (`active | retired`), `owner` (`^design/[0-9]{2}$` resolving to one file), `retired_in` (SemVer `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` equal to base `crates/grdr-schema/Cargo.toml` `version` on retirement), and acyclic `replaced_by` (`active` in head when set on transition, preserving historical `A -> B -> C` chains). Every refusal class (1 to 13) has an explicit fixture and stops before writing `generated.rs`. `history()` enforces no deletion, immutable `code`/`severity`/`scopes`/`surface`/`stale`, one-way `active -> retired`, and immutable retirement fields, failing closed (`"could not look"`) on unreadable inputs.
5. **`"Content, not surface"` rule for later conditions (section 6).** Later condition additions follow RFC 0007 section 4 (`"Content, not surface"`) rather than opening a fresh RFC per code, while remaining `tier:high` (`schema/**`, `crates/grdr-schema/**`) with `drift`, `history`, `grdr-interface-gen` tokens, `/codes/<code>` pages (`F2`), and `docs/runbooks/` coverage (`F7` for `degraded`/`critical`). `#476` (`spool-unwritable`) keeps its own RFC because `#476` D2 to D4 alter receipt-ring and `health.gap` behaviour.
6. **Initial slice-0 entries (section 7).** Exactly eleven active seed entries (`child-unresponsive`, `engine-down`, `engine-restarting`, `engine-sandbox-partial`, `failsafe-publish-failed`, `floor-missing`, `persistent-key-corrupt`, `persistent-key-unreadable`, `safe-mode`, `sandbox-violation`, `stale-bundle`), verifying surface-state orthogonality (`safe-mode` is the sole `surface = "safe-mode"` entry; `floor-missing` and `sandbox-violation` are `critical` with `surface = "none"`; `stale-bundle` is `warn` with `stale = true`) and excluding `spool-unwritable` (`#476`).
7. **Tokens, docgen, data classes, assurance and stability (section 8).** Sequencing follows [RFC 0010](../../rfcs/0010-code-tokens-before-the-token-check.md) R1 to R5 (`#2056` is blocked only by `#668` and `#278`; `#2057` authors tokens for `grdr_schema::health::ALL` at its merge base once `#2056`, `#1613` and `#1853` land, enforcing `git merge-base --is-ancestor origin/main <head>` across the window). All registry keys and `grdr_schema::health` values are `structural` (`P8`); assurance effect is none (`P1`); `grdr-schema` Rust API class is `experimental` (`surfaces.toml`) while condition wire semantics (`code`, `severity`, `scopes`, `surface`, `stale`, R5) are `stable` across releases.

**No pin, no evidence, no index file edit.** RFC 0016 decides no pinned path, so `rfcs/pins.toml` stays unchanged (`docs/design/00-decisions.md` at `a80859235e7a2a4444648d6fa8d389d78dde6be9`) and nothing is added under `rfcs/evidence/` ([RFC 0001](../../rfcs/0001-adopt-the-spine.md#design); [ADR-0036](ADR-0036-the-amended-spine-is-the-baseline-adopted-by-rfc-0001.md)). `docs/decisions/README.md` and `rfcs/README.md` do not exist in the repository (`ADR-0045` and `ADR-0046` touched neither), and `rfcs/0007-grdr-model-and-grdr-schema-slice-0-surface.md` is not edited.

**What this acceptance binds.** The `schema/health.toml` grammar, refusals, `history` invariants, `"Content, not surface"` rule, `grdr_schema::health` signatures, token id scheme (`health.<code>.{meaning,cause,fix}`), `codes/<code>` anchor, and the eleven slice-0 condition semantics change only by a new RFC naming RFC 0016 (and RFC 0007 where its sections 2 R10, 4 or 8 are affected). Adding or retiring condition entries under sections 5 and 6 is content on the `high` lane (`eval-review` + `eval-security` on two distinct models/providers, plus CI `drift` and `history`).

## Enforced by
- **Type-system sealing and parity (`#2056`):** `generated::Seal(())` prevents external or non-generated construction of `HealthCode`, `HealthEntry`, `Retirement` or `ConditionState::Retired`; `find()` returns `Option<&'static HealthEntry>` only; `cases` and `compile_fail` doctests verify field-by-field parity and sealing.
- **Modular `layout` and manifest hardening (`#2056`):** `tests/construction.rs` `layout` (dep-info + token scan) permits only `lib.rs` and active `{codes, limits, health}` 3-file triplets; `crates/grdr-schema/Cargo.toml` enforces `build = false`, `autotests = false`, zero normal dependencies, and `#![no_std]` / `wasm32-unknown-unknown` compatibility.
- **Deterministic `drift` and hardened `history` (`#2056`, `#2058`, `#1943`):** `tests/health_gen.rs` `drift` runs in `cargo test --workspace --locked` and CI; `.github/workflows/schema-health-history.yml` runs base-built `history()` on bounded regular-file head blobs (`permissions: contents: read`, failing closed with `"could not look"`).
- **Tokens, reference pages, and runbooks (`#2057`, `#1636`, `#1644`, `#1629`):** `grdr-interface-gen` walks `grdr_schema::health::ALL` (`GRDR-IFG-*`), `grdr-docgen codes` emits `/codes/<code>` pages (`F2`), and `docs-check.mjs --check F7` verifies runbooks for every active `degraded` and `critical` code.
- **Review lane:** Until `#2058` lands in `security/trust-core.paths`, `schema/**` and `crates/grdr-schema/**` are already `high` in [`.claude/risk-tiers.toml`](../../.claude/risk-tiers.toml) (`eval-review` + `eval-security` on two distinct models/providers).

## Consequences
- `#147`, `#476`, `#1636`, `#1644`, and the slice-0 first-run path items (`#149`, `#421`, `#427`, `#1538`, `#1673`) gain one frozen registry path (`schema/health.toml`), one sealed Rust surface (`grdr_schema::health`), and eleven frozen seed conditions.
- `#2056` proceeds immediately after `#668` and `#278` without waiting on `#448`, `#1613`, `#1853`, or `#2057`, while `#2057` closes the source-token check and enforces RFC 0010 R5 across any concurrent `schema/health.toml` PR.
- Because `severity`, `scopes`, `surface`, and `stale` are immutable once on `main` (R5), correcting a released entry requires retiring that code (`retired_in`, `replaced_by`) and introducing a new condition code.
- Re-pointing `blockedBy` on `#147`, `#476`, `#1636`, and `#1644` from `#668` to `#2056` keeps them blocked until both `#668` and `#2056` land.

## Alternatives rejected
- Those of [RFC 0016, Alternatives](../../rfcs/0016-health-condition-registry.md#alternatives): placing the registry at `crates/grdr-schema/health.toml`, requiring a fresh RFC per condition code, exposing `HealthCode::from_str(&str)` or string literals, and emitting timer-driven `health.sensor` heartbeats on the `decision` lane.
- Adding pin records or committed evidence files under `rfcs/evidence/`: RFC 0016 changes no pinned path ([RFC 0001](../../rfcs/0001-adopt-the-spine.md#design); [ADR-0036](ADR-0036-the-amended-spine-is-the-baseline-adopted-by-rfc-0001.md)).
- Editing reviewed RFC 0016 Design prose or editing accepted RFC 0007 directly: RFC 0001 ("RFC states", "Review binds the decision") forbids modifying reviewed body sections on acceptance or editing an accepted RFC in place.

## Revisit when
- Any change is needed to `schema/health.toml` keys, validation rules, R5 immutability invariants, `grdr_schema::health` signatures or enums, token id format, or `grdr-schema`'s `experimental` stability class; this requires a new amending RFC naming RFC 0016 (and RFC 0007 where sections 2 R10, 4 or 8 change).
- `#476` is decided, defining receipt-ring and `health.gap` rules and preparing `spool-unwritable` to join `schema/health.toml` as content.
- `grdr-schema` promotes out of `experimental`, requiring a promoting RFC under `D-60` and [ADR-0020](ADR-0020-stability-classes-live-in-surfaces-toml.md).
