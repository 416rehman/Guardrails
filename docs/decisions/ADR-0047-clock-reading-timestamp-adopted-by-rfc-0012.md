## Context
During RFC 0008's review on #1924, Seat 3 (`persona-soc-analyst`, comment `7081910895`) and Seat 9 (`persona-intl`, comment `7081901532`) identified a wall-clock representation gap on `grdr_ports::retry::ClassReading`. `ClassReading.at`, `HealthReading.at` and `OpenBreaker::until()` (written in RFC 0008 as `Breaker::Open { until }` before RFC 0009 section 16 introduced `OpenBreaker`) hold `Monotonic` values, which are `u64` nanoseconds from an arbitrary per-process origin and never cross process boundaries or render as calendar dates. Without a UTC wall-clock anchor paired atomically with `at`, the uplink (#1238), `grdr doctor` (#1289), server device coverage-gap rows (#1972), OCSF/SIEM exports and adapter set-up readings cannot show when a reading or health failure happened or convert `until` into a locale-formatted wait duration, while RFC 0008 section 3 left UTC implicit on `Clock::now()`. Under [ADR-0040](ADR-0040-clock-entropy-and-the-retry-executor-adopted-by-rfc-0008.md) constraints `4a` and `1e`, #1963 was filed as RFC 0008 amendment 1, blocking #283, #1238 and #1966 (`amendment 2`) before readying ([RFC 0012, Motivation](../../rfcs/0012-clock-amendment-1-class-reading-timestamp.md#motivation)).

RFC 0012 landed on `main` in `State: Review` via pull request #2037 (head commit `0ea031e1ba602ecbd0117a3d9134570f33983f54`, merged at `a0d9d0b423f420752f5aab735f43f65af867d6af`; reviewed RFC file blob `950d69b0682d010924537d47a75f4718754fe787`, SHA-256 `db954f08c1858d3b5a4a200e0cf171f353f50a8e5275dc993262210b3e336c34`), written against the spine pin in force `a80859235e7a2a4444648d6fa8d389d78dde6be9` (SHA-256 `8f6ea876777c0121ecec068c0bc4cef45932be45c1c2aff4140712db1e06628d`). `rfcs/0008-clock-entropy-and-the-retry-executor.md` remains untouched at reviewed blob `b338780823870d0f81c92e1a6f9cd2a5ec472eb8` and accepted blob `b4af2b007041e3abdf6f7439824662ea6f08c52d` ([ADR-0040](ADR-0040-clock-entropy-and-the-retry-executor-adopted-by-rfc-0008.md)).

## Decision
**Accepted.** RFC 0012 (amendment 1 of RFC 0008) is accepted on reviewed RFC file blob `950d69b0682d010924537d47a75f4718754fe787` (SHA-256 `db954f08c1858d3b5a4a200e0cf171f353f50a8e5275dc993262210b3e336c34`) against spine pin `a80859235e7a2a4444648d6fa8d389d78dde6be9` ([RFC 0012, Design](../../rfcs/0012-clock-amendment-1-class-reading-timestamp.md#design)). In outline:
- **R1 (`ClassReading` layout).** `grdr_ports::retry::ClassReading` gains `pub taken: Timestamp` (`data_class = structural`) and holds exactly six fields (`at: Monotonic`, `taken: Timestamp`, `breaker: Breaker`, `health: Option<HealthReading>`, `attempt_counter: AttemptCounter`, `last_code: Option<Code>`), retaining `#[derive(Clone, Copy, Debug, PartialEq, Eq)]` and excluding `failure_share()`, `at_cap()` and `abandoned()`.
- **R2 (atomic snapshot under one mutex hold).** `RetryState::reading(&self) -> ClassReading` acquires the state `std::sync::Mutex` once (reclaiming a poisoned lock with `PoisonError::into_inner`) and reads `at = self.clock.monotonic()` and `taken = self.clock.now()` inside that same lock hold alongside copying `breaker`, `health`, `attempt_counter` and `last_code`. `run` and `run_until` keep reading `Clock::monotonic()` only, and `HealthReading` keeps its exact three fields unchanged.
- **R3 (`Clock::now()` UTC semantics).** `grdr_ports::clock::Clock::now(&self) -> Timestamp` returns the wall-clock instant in UTC (`1970-01-01T00:00:00Z`, `Timestamp::MIN..=Timestamp::MAX`), never a local time-zone offset or formatted string; `now()` may jump forwards or backwards; and no `Clock` implementation derives `monotonic()` from `now()` or `now()` from `monotonic()`. `clock::suite()` in `grdr-ports-testkit` keeps its existing three cases in layer 2 (`ARCH-003`).
- **R4 (wire, IPC, persisted counter and export conversion).** `Monotonic` instants never leave the owning process. Callers convert past instants (`HealthReading.at`) via `reading.taken.checked_sub(reading.at.saturating_duration_since(instant))` (`None` treated as unavailable without panicking or wrapping). Derived IPC, fleet wire (`CheckInRequest.health` under its own D-61 wire RFC), `status.pb` and OCSF/SIEM exports carry UTC `Timestamp` and relative durations (`structural`). Persisted attempt-counter files stay counters-only. Logs recording `reading.taken` use `LogSafe` (`LogValue::Timestamp`) under RFC 0009 constraint `1a`.
- **R5 (UI, CLI and console rendering).** `taken` and converted `Timestamp`s travel in `MessageRef`s or typed view-models and render via `:datetime` in the viewer's configured time zone with UTC one step away (machine-readable `--json` keeps UTC `Timestamp`). `Breaker::Open`'s `until` (`OpenBreaker::until()`) converts to a non-negative `Duration` via `open.until().saturating_duration_since(reading.at)` (or `until.saturating_duration_since(clock.monotonic())` for `RetryError::BreakerOpen`, `Duration::ZERO` when `until <= reading.at`) and renders via `:grdr:duration`, never as a wall-clock deadline and only in Advanced and diagnostics views.

**No pin and no evidence.** RFC 0012 decides no pinned path, so its Decision section holds no decision line, `rfcs/pins.toml` is unchanged, nothing is added under `rfcs/evidence/`, and `rfcs/0008-clock-entropy-and-the-retry-executor.md` is not edited ([RFC 0001, "RFC states"](../../rfcs/0001-adopt-the-spine.md#design); [ADR-0036](ADR-0036-the-amended-spine-is-the-baseline-adopted-by-rfc-0001.md), "Evidence added in route"; [ADR-0040](ADR-0040-clock-entropy-and-the-retry-executor-adopted-by-rfc-0008.md)). The accepted RFC 0012 file differs from `950d69b` only in `State:`, `Decision record:` and its `Decision` section.

**Constraints.** The decision binds the downstream rules of [RFC 0012, Design R6](../../rfcs/0012-clock-amendment-1-class-reading-timestamp.md#design) across the named work items:
1. **`#283` (`retry::run` in `grdr-ports`):** implement `ClassReading` with R1's exact six `structural` fields; implement `RetryState::reading(&self)` reading `monotonic()` and `now()` inside the same `Mutex` hold (with poisoned-lock recovery and `run`/`run_until` free of `now()`); and add tests in `crates/grdr-ports-testkit/tests/retry*.rs` proving snapshot accuracy without mutation, `ManualClock::jump_wall` invariance of `at`/`health.at`/relative `until`, `checked_sub` UTC recovery after `ManualClock::advance` plus `Timestamp::MIN` `None` safety, and poisoned-mutex recovery.
2. **`#1238` (`22-5`, `uplink-reconnect`):** keep `Monotonic` off `grdr.fleet.v1alpha1`, `grdr.local.v1alpha1` and `MessageRef`, keep persisted counter files counters-only (`2f`), convert `HealthReading.at` via `reading.taken.checked_sub(reading.at.saturating_duration_since(health.at))` (`None` omitted/unavailable) for `CheckIn` health metadata and exports (`Timestamp`, UTC, `structural`), convert `OpenBreaker::until()` to `open.until().saturating_duration_since(reading.at)` (`Duration`/`Millis`, `structural`) for `MessageRef`s and diagnostics (never wall-clock text, never formatted in `grdr-ports` or `grdr-uplink`, and excluded from the Simple view), and test `jump_wall` invariance and zero `Monotonic` output.
3. **`#281` and `#1927` (`Clock`):** `#281` documents UTC semantics on `grdr_ports::clock::Clock::now` (`R3`) while keeping `clock::suite()`'s three tests unchanged; `#1927` (`OsClock::now()`) already converts `SystemTime::now()` to clamped UTC `Timestamp` (`AC-5`) and needs no spec change.
4. **`#1966` (`RFC 0008 amendment 2`):** unblocked from `#1963` upon merge of `#1963`'s decision PR; its scoped `set_up` entry point returns `ClassReading` with all six fields of R1 (`taken` read in the same lock hold as `at`), and its R6 prose correction for `reading()` names `at` and `taken` alongside `breaker`, `health`, `attempt_counter` and `last_code`.
5. **`#1289` (`grdr doctor`), `#30`, `#1540` and `#1972` (server coverage-gap row):** `#1289` (and `#30`/`#1540` where class readings are shown) reads `ClassReading.taken` via `:datetime` (UTC `Timestamp` in `--json`) and renders `OpenBreaker::until()` (and cached `status.pb` or set-up snapshots) as a relative duration from `taken` via `:grdr:duration` without mutating state (RFC 0008 constraint `2b`) and outside the Simple view (constraint `3`); `#1289` still brings `state_word` by its own amendment of RFC 0009. `#1972` uses the UTC `Timestamp` (`taken` / converted `since`) from `#1238`'s `CheckIn` health metadata for its coverage-gap `since` column, rendered via `:datetime`.
6. **RFC 0002 storage suites, `#1967` and `#1970`:** `#1967` (`architecture.toml`) and `#1970` (`docs/design/*`) take no changes for RFC 0012; storage adapters' kept set-up `ClassReading` (`Fixture::setup_reading` and `FixtureError::Open`) gains `taken: Timestamp` automatically without changing RFC 0002 signatures, rendering set-up `taken` via `:datetime` and `until` via `:grdr:duration`.

**What this acceptance binds.** `ClassReading`'s six-field layout, `RetryState::reading()`'s single-hold `(at, taken)` snapshot rule, `Clock::now()`'s UTC semantics, and the process-local `Monotonic` and relative-duration rendering rules are part of the decided `grdr_ports::retry` and `grdr_ports::clock` surface alongside [ADR-0040](ADR-0040-clock-entropy-and-the-retry-executor-adopted-by-rfc-0008.md) and change only by a later RFC naming both RFC 0008 and RFC 0012.

## Enforced by
- **Compile-time and API checks (`#283`, `#1741`):** `ClassReading` exposes `pub taken: Timestamp` alongside its existing five fields; `RetryState::reading(&self)` requires `C: Clock`.
- **`#283` and `#1238` tests:** `ManualClock::jump_wall` and `ManualClock::advance` unit/integration tests verify atomic same-hold `(at, taken)` snapshots, `Timestamp::MIN` `None` safety, poisoned-mutex recovery, relative `until` duration invariance, counters-only counter persistence, and zero `Monotonic` emission across `CheckIn`, `MessageRef` and exports.
- **`#1927` AC-5:** `OsClock::now()` agrees with `SystemTime::now()` in UTC within 1 s and clamps out-of-range times cleanly (`wall_out_of_range`).
- **Interface and code review (`eval-ux`, `eval-review`, `eval-security`):** verifies `self.clock.monotonic()` and `self.clock.now()` share one `Mutex` hold in `RetryState::reading()` (`#283`), and verifies `:datetime` / `:grdr:duration` usage and the Simple-vs-Advanced boundary on `#1853`, `#1289` and `#1972` (D-69, UX3 to UX6).

## Consequences
- Unblocks `#1966` (`RFC 0008 amendment 2`) from `#1963` upon merge of `#1963`'s decision PR, and clears `#1963`'s prerequisite on `#283`, `#1238`, `#1289` and `#1972` (`#283` and `#1238` continue to wait for `#1966` per ADR-0040 constraint `4b`).
- Callers constructing or exhaustively pattern-matching `ClassReading` without `..` must handle `pub taken: Timestamp` (allowed under `experimental`, D-60 / ADR-0020; zero workspace callers have landed yet).
- `RetryState::reading()` performs two synchronous non-blocking clock reads (`monotonic()` and `now()`) inside its mutex hold, while `run` and `run_until` keep reading `monotonic()` only.
- `HealthReading.at` conversion (`taken.checked_sub(at.saturating_duration_since(health.at))`) assumes no wall-clock step occurred between `health.at` and `reading()`, and surfaces handle `None` gracefully near `Timestamp::MIN`; UI/CLI surfaces avoid NTP/VM clock-jump falsehoods on open breakers by displaying relative `until` durations from `taken`.

## Alternatives rejected
- Those of [RFC 0012, Alternatives](../../rfcs/0012-clock-amendment-1-class-reading-timestamp.md#alternatives).
- Adding a pin record or evidence files: RFC 0012 decides no pinned path, so adding either is out of route ([RFC 0001](../../rfcs/0001-adopt-the-spine.md#design); [ADR-0036](ADR-0036-the-amended-spine-is-the-baseline-adopted-by-rfc-0001.md)).
- Editing `rfcs/0008-clock-entropy-and-the-retry-executor.md` directly or modifying RFC 0012's reviewed `Summary`, `Motivation`, `Design` or `Surfaces changed`: RFC 0001 ("RFC states", "Review binds the decision") and RFC 0008 constraint `4d` prohibit editing an accepted RFC or changing reviewed body text after review.

## Revisit when
- A defect is found in RFC 0012's `ClassReading` layout, `RetryState::reading()` snapshot contract, `Clock::now()` UTC specification or relative-duration `until` rendering rule, which opens a new amending RFC naming both RFC 0008 and RFC 0012 (`docs/design/30` section 3).
- `#1966` (RFC 0008 amendment 2) is decided, adding `Permille`, the `set_up` entry point returning RFC 0012's six-field `ClassReading`, offline state and suspend/start-up wait rules alongside RFC 0012.
- `grdr-ports` promotes out of `experimental`, requiring a promoting amendment per [ADR-0040](ADR-0040-clock-entropy-and-the-retry-executor-adopted-by-rfc-0008.md).
