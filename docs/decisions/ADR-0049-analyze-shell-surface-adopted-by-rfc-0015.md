## Context
WI-0019 (#22) adds the first normaliser in `grdr-core`, the POSIX shell canonicaliser of [`docs/design/13`](../design/13-detection-engine-and-content.md) section 8, alongside integration tests and the differential fuzz target required by D-10 and [ADR-0009](ADR-0009-normalisers-are-tri-state-with-differential-targets.md). Because `grdr-core` is a layer 1 public crate (`experimental` in [`surfaces.toml`](../../surfaces.toml) under [ADR-0037](ADR-0037-the-fast-path-rust-surface-adopted-by-rfc-0005.md)), its public surface changes only by RFC (D-61). Before RFC 0015, [`crates/grdr-core/src/lib.rs`](../../crates/grdr-core/src/lib.rs) kept `mod analyze;` private, escalation `#22-E1` held `#415` (`brush-parser` and `libfuzzer-sys`) and `#422` to `#426`, closed `#416` referenced pre-ADR-0029 `.agents/owners.toml` and free-form `[analyze.shell]` TOML incompatible with RFC 0007 ([ADR-0038](ADR-0038-the-grdr-model-and-grdr-schema-slice-0-surface-adopted-by-rfc-0007.md)), and the downstream specs of `#422`, `#423`, `#424` and `#426` diverged on `ShellDialect`, `Command::Subshell` redirects, `Pipeline` non-emptiness, `ListConnector` tail semantics, `Debug` redaction and `ShellLimits` bounds.

## Decision
**Accepted.** The slice-0 public surface of `grdr_core::analyze` and `grdr_core::analyze::shell` is RFC 0015 at pre-acceptance reviewed blob `c97afd1ad243faf9f9a05755e630aad54a0b3137` (SHA-256 `6bbf15ae532229e5de81dd5aa500783cff87fc60982e0a2a4a4b13a4d7354973`), written against spine pin `a80859235e7a2a4444648d6fa8d389d78dde6be9` (SHA-256 `8f6ea876777c0121ecec068c0bc4cef45932be45c1c2aff4140712db1e06628d`) ([RFC 0015, Design](../../rfcs/0015-analyze-shell-surface.md#design)). In outline:

1. **Tri-state resolution, AST surface and `experimental` stability (R1).**
   - `crates/grdr-core/src/lib.rs` exports `pub mod analyze;`, keeping internal submodules private and re-exporting each item at one canonical path (P17). `grdr-core` keeps its `experimental` stability class in [`surfaces.toml`](../../surfaces.toml) (`[crate]` row from RFC 0005).
   - `grdr_core::analyze` exposes `Span` (`u32` byte offsets for native/wasm32 parity), exhaustive `LimitKind` (`InputBytes`, `EnvBytes`, `ExpandedBytes`, `NestingDepth`, `Words`), exhaustive `UnresolvedKind` and `UnresolvedReason` (13 variants), `#[non_exhaustive]` `MalformedReason` (4 variants implementing `grdr_schema::codes::Coded` and `grdr_model::LogSafe`), and `Resolution<T>` (`Resolved(T)`, `Unresolved(Vec<UnresolvedReason>)` guaranteed non-empty on every analyser return, `Malformed(MalformedReason)`), with no `Default`, no `Display`, and no variant conversion.
   - `grdr_core::analyze::shell` exposes `ShellDialect` (`Posix`, `PowerShell`, `Cmd`), `ShellInput`, `ShellLimits` (`from_schema()` reading `grdr_schema::limits::ANALYZE_SHELL_*` plus validated `new(...)`, no `Default`), `#[non_exhaustive]` `ShellLimitsError`, `ListConnector`, `CommandTree`, `ListItem`, `Pipeline` (non-empty by construction via private `Vec<Command>`), `Command` (`Simple(SimpleCommand)` and `Subshell { tree: CommandTree, redirects: Vec<Redirect> }`), `SimpleCommand`, `Assignment`, `RedirectOp`, `Redirect` (`fd: u32` with POSIX.1-2024 §2.7 defaults), and `canonicalise(&ShellInput, &ShellLimits) -> Resolution<CommandTree>`.

2. **Module placement and cross-crate rule (R2).**
   - `Resolution<T>`, `UnresolvedReason` and `MalformedReason` remain in `grdr_core::analyze` for slice 0. Future `design/13` section 8 analysers inside `grdr-core::analyze` (E2.3 path canonicaliser, E2.4 host/URL classifier) share them directly, and `grdr_fastpath::Resolution<T>` stays separate (`#![no_std]`, no `alloc`, `Absent` variant).
   - If a separate layer 1 crate (`grdr-llm-proto`, `grdr-mcp-proto`) later needs a tri-state normaliser type or `analyze` splits into `grdr-analyze` under [`docs/design/03`](../design/03-system-architecture-and-modularity.md) section 2, a future RFC handles that promotion or split without adding a same-layer edge to `grdr-core`.

3. **Error-code registry and `limits.toml` integration (R3, RFC 0007, RFC 0010).**
   - `schema/codes/areas.toml` defines `[area.ANALYZE]` (`owner = "design/13"`), and `schema/codes/analyze.toml` registers `GRDR-ANALYZE-0001`..`0004` for `MalformedReason` and `GRDR-ANALYZE-0005`..`0006` for `ShellLimitsError`, bound via `grdr_schema::codes::Coded` with no `"GRDR-"` literal in `grdr-core` (`ARCH-017`). Language token registration (`code.analyze_0001`..`0006`) follows [RFC 0010](../../rfcs/0010-code-tokens-before-the-token-check.md) ([ADR-0044](ADR-0044-code-tokens-before-the-token-check-adopted-by-rfc-0010.md)).
   - Five `[bound.analyze.shell.*]` rows (`input-max`, `env-max`, `expanded-max`, `nesting-depth-max`, `words-max`) live in `limits.toml` and enter `grdr_core::analyze::shell` solely through `grdr_schema::limits` constants (`ARCH-019`, D-69, P14), tracked in #2053.

4. **Parser admission, `ARCH-007` encapsulation and layer 1 purity (R4, `#22-E1`).**
   - Escalation `#22-E1` is settled by Option A: `brush-parser` (MIT, `default-features = false`) is admitted as a private normal dependency of `grdr-core`, and `libfuzzer-sys` (MIT OR Apache-2.0) is admitted for the out-of-workspace `fuzz/` crate, both via #415 subject to [`supply-chain/licences.toml`](../../supply-chain/licences.toml), `cargo deny check` and `wasm32-unknown-unknown` compilation. `grdr-core` also imports `grdr-schema` (layer 0) and `grdr-model` (layer 1, existing `[[same_layer_rule]]` in [`architecture.toml`](../../architecture.toml)) while `analyze` imports no sibling module of `grdr-core` (`ARCH-013`).
   - Zero `brush_parser` types, traits, errors or `From`/`TryFrom` conversions cross the public boundary (`ARCH-007`), allowing replacement by an internal pure-Rust POSIX parser (including if `#415` AC-2 finds `wasm32` incompatibility) with zero RFC and zero public API change.
   - `analyze` performs no I/O, reads no filesystem/env/clock/entropy, uses `BTreeMap`/`BTreeSet` for cross-target determinism, compiles for `wasm32-unknown-unknown`, and enforces workspace no-panic and `arithmetic_side_effects = "deny"` lints.

5. **Canonicalisation, privacy (`P8`) and security invariants.**
   - Dialect dispatch rejects non-POSIX dialects (`PowerShell`, `Cmd`) immediately with `Unresolved(vec![UnresolvedReason::UnsupportedDialect { .. }])` prior to parsing until `#894` (E2.2).
   - Pre-parse bounds check `InputBytes` and checked-sum `EnvBytes`, lowering bounds enforce `NestingDepth`, `Words` and incremental checked `ExpandedBytes` (preventing Billion-Laughs expansion OOM), invalid UTF-8 and NUL bytes return `Unresolved(UnsupportedConstruct)` (`#425` R4), subshell redirects lower into `Command::Subshell { tree, redirects }` without leaking child-scope assignments, lowered `argv[0]` refuses state-mutating built-ins, prefixed POSIX special built-ins and `command`/`builtin` wrappers, and unquoted globs in `argv[0]` or `Redirect.target` return `UnsupportedConstruct`.
   - `content` fields (`ShellInput.command`, `ShellInput.env`, `argv`, `Assignment`, `Redirect.target`) redact to byte lengths or entry counts in manual `fmt::Debug` and never implement `LogSafe`, while `UnresolvedReason`, `MalformedReason`, `ShellLimitsError`, `LimitKind`, `UnresolvedKind` and `ShellDialect` carry structural metadata and implement `LogSafe`.

6. **No pin and no evidence.** RFC 0015 decides no pinned path. [`rfcs/pins.toml`](../../rfcs/pins.toml) stays unchanged at `docs/design/00-decisions.md` blob `a80859235e7a2a4444648d6fa8d389d78dde6be9` (`sha256` `8f6ea876777c0121ecec068c0bc4cef45932be45c1c2aff4140712db1e06628d`), and no file is added under `rfcs/evidence/` ([RFC 0001](../../rfcs/0001-adopt-the-spine.md#design); [ADR-0036](ADR-0036-the-amended-spine-is-the-baseline-adopted-by-rfc-0001.md)).

7. **What this acceptance binds.** Adding, removing or changing any public item, variant, signature or canonicalisation contract in `grdr_core::analyze` or `grdr_core::analyze::shell`, moving or splitting `Resolution<T>` across crates, or promoting `grdr-core` out of `experimental` requires a new RFC naming RFC 0015. Adding further error codes in `schema/codes/analyze.toml` follows RFC 0007 section 4 ("Content, not surface") without amending RFC 0015. Replacing `brush-parser` privately under `ARCH-007` requires zero RFC.

## Consequences
- `#415`, `#422` to `#426`, `#2053` and `#2054` share one decided public surface, one error-code area (`ANALYZE`) and one `limits.toml` schema contract (`[bound.analyze.shell.*]` via `grdr_schema::limits`).
- Replacing `brush-parser` (for example if `#415` AC-2 finds a `wasm32` blocker) stays entirely behind `grdr_core::analyze::shell::canonicalise` without another RFC (`ARCH-007`).
- Non-POSIX shell commands (`PowerShell`, `Cmd`) fail closed into deterministic `Unresolved(UnsupportedDialect)` on Windows and cross-platform harnesses until `#894` implements E2.2.
- Adding variants to exhaustive `LimitKind`, `UnresolvedKind` or `UnresolvedReason` for E2.2, E2.3 or E2.4 while `grdr-core` is `experimental` will intentionally require updating internal matches in `grdr-core` and `tests/shell_ref` under an amending RFC.
