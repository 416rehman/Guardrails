# D01 time-assumptions (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
Find wall-clock reads outside `Clock`, timestamps used for ordering, TTL arithmetic that breaks under skew or suspend, and any expiry that stops enforcement.

Looks in: every adapter; D-37's rule that expiry marks a device Stale and never stops enforcement; ordering by epoch, lane and sequence.

Evidence: the clock read or timestamp comparison, and what depends on it.

Claim: "P reads the wall clock outside `Clock`", "orders by timestamp", "TTL T breaks when the clock skews or the host suspends", or "expiry E stops enforcement". Use of `Clock`, sequence ordering or a monotonic bound refutes it.

Class keys: `time:wall-clock`, `time:timestamp-ordering`, `time:skew`, `time:suspend`, `time:expiry-stops-enforcement`.

Artefact: list.

Closure guidance: a lint (`disallowed-methods` on wall-clock reads) or a type for the time value.
