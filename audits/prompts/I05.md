# I05 agent-kit-supply-chain (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
For every plugin, skill, hook and MCP server the development fleet loads: is it pinned, does the shipped artefact match its source, what hooks and servers does it install, and is it past the cooldown?

Looks in: the private marketplace manifest, `.agents/`, harness settings. The plugins `.agents/kit/marketplace.toml` enables by owner instruction (2026-11-11) have the cooldown waived: record the waiver in `checked_and_sound`, not as a result. Anything that calls another vendor's model is a result, because the model rule allows none.

Evidence: the manifest entry and its pin, and the hooks or servers the item installs.

Claim: "Kit item K is unpinned, its artefact differs from source S, it installs hook or server H that no manifest declares, it calls another vendor's model, or it is inside its cooldown without a waiver." The pin, a matching digest, the declaration or the waiver refutes it.

Class keys: `kit-chain:unpinned`, `kit-chain:artefact-mismatch`, `kit-chain:undeclared-hook`, `kit-chain:undeclared-server`, `kit-chain:other-vendor-model`, `kit-chain:cooldown`.

Artefact: kit inventory.

Closure guidance: a lint in `kit check`, or a decision record.
