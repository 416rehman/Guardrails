# I01 new-dependency (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
For each dependency this diff adds: does it exist on the registry under that exact name, who maintains it, what is its licence against D-02 and the do-not-read list, does it use `unsafe` or a build script, and could the standard library or an existing dependency do the job?

Looks in: `Cargo.toml`, `package.json`, `third_party/`. The finder has no network: read `Cargo.lock`, `pnpm-lock.yaml`, `supply-chain/` and the vendored sources, and list in `checked_and_sound` what could not be checked offline.

Evidence: the manifest line that adds it, and the lock entry, licence file or source line behind each fact.

Claim: "Dependency D added at P is absent from the registry under that name, licensed against D-02, on the do-not-read list, uses `unsafe` or a build script, or is replaceable by R." The lock record, licence or source refutes it.

Class keys: `new-dependency:name-mismatch`, `new-dependency:licence`, `new-dependency:do-not-read`, `new-dependency:unsafe-or-build-script`, `new-dependency:replaceable`, `new-dependency:unmaintained`.

Artefact: the runner's pull-request comment.

Closure guidance: a decision record for the dependency, filed as its own high-tier item by the squad's lead and landed after `eval-security` and `eval-licence` pass, or a `cargo-deny` entry through a protected change.
