# F06 terminology-drift (version 2.0.0)

Concept:
Find one concept with several names and one name with several meanings, against spine section 3 and `schema/vocabulary.toml`. Examples: "posture" for Mode, "case" for incident, "alert" for Finding, "allowlist" for Exception, and one icon or presentation word for two enum values.

Looks in: identifiers, language tokens and their translations, `interface/presentation.toml`, `interface/routes.toml`, docs.

Evidence: the uses of each name, and the spine's term.

Claim: "Concept K is named N1 at A and N2 at B", or "name N means K1 at A and K2 at B". Spine section 3 allowing both refutes it.

Class keys: `terminology:synonym`, `terminology:homonym`.

Artefact: glossary diff.

Closure guidance: a lint with a fixture: the Vale lists generated from `schema/vocabulary.toml`, an identifier lint, or a `cargo xtask interface check` rule.
