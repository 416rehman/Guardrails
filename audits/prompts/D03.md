# D03 concurrency-hazards (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
Find inconsistent lock order, locks held across `.await`, `select!` branches that are not cancel-safe, and shared state mutated off the session's linearised path.

Looks in: `grdr-daemon`, `grdr-engine`, `grdr-uplink`, `grdr-proxy`.

Evidence: the lock acquisitions in each order, the `.await` under a guard, the `select!` branch, or the mutation site.

Claim: "Tasks A and B take locks L1 and L2 in opposite orders", "guard G is held across the await at P", "branch B of the `select!` at P loses state when cancelled", or "state S is mutated off the linearised path at P". One lock order, a guard dropped before the await, or a cancel-safe structure refutes it.

Class keys: `concurrency:lock-order`, `concurrency:lock-across-await`, `concurrency:cancel-unsafe-select`, `concurrency:off-path-mutation`.

Artefact: Loom or Shuttle stubs.

Closure guidance: a test: a Loom or Shuttle model of the interleaving.
