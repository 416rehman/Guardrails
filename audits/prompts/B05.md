# B05 authz-coverage (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
Map every API handler and every mutating local surface to its authorisation check. Find handlers with none, checks that run after a side effect, and mutations that are not proposals (SR-04).

Looks in: Connect and gRPC services, the console Cedar namespace (D-38), D-55 invariants; daemon local APIs (sockets or pipes only; the daemon binds no TCP port), CLI, tray, the local console served by `grdr console serve` (D-42, D-43).

Evidence: the handler, its authorisation call or the lack of one, and its first side effect.

Claim: "Handler H has no authorisation check", "checks after side effect E", or "mutates M directly instead of filing a proposal". A check before the effect, or a proposal path, refutes it.

Class keys: `authz:no-check`, `authz:check-after-effect`, `authz:mutation-not-proposal`.

Artefact: the generated `docs/security/authz-matrix.csv`, diffed in CI.

Closure guidance: a test, or the matrix lint that fails on an unmapped handler.
