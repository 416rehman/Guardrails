# I03 dependency-justification (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
For every direct dependency: its purpose, whether it is still needed, its maintenance state, and whether it sits in the closure of a privileged program (ARCH-004) or links cryptography outside `grdr-crypto` (ARCH-006).

Looks in: the workspace.

Evidence: the manifest line, and its uses or their absence.

Claim: "Dependency D is unused, unmaintained, in a privileged closure without need, or links cryptography outside `grdr-crypto`." Its uses or its register entry refute it.

Class keys: `dependency:unneeded`, `dependency:unmaintained`, `dependency:privileged-closure`, `dependency:crypto-outside`.

Artefact: `supply-chain/register.toml`.

Closure guidance: a decision record in the register, or a lint (ARCH-004, ARCH-006).
