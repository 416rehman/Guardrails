# Y01 frozen-replay (version 1.0.0)

Procedure:
No wording of its own, and no model session runs under this id. The runner replays A01, A06, C07, E02 and M01 to M05 with `--frozen` on the pinned commits of `audit-bench`, each with its own wording, models and caps, and diffs the results against the previous model's.

Looks in: `audit-bench`.

Output: recall and precision delta per prompt, as run records under each replayed prompt's id, linked to this run.

Closure guidance: a decision record per prompt: keep, retune or retire. Audits do not adopt the new model while any replayed prompt loses more than 10 points of recall (target).
