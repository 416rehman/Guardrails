# C06 retry-safety (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
Find operations that are unsafe to retry or run twice, and retries with no backoff, jitter, budget or idempotency key.

Looks in: uplink batches on `(device, epoch, lane, seq)`, single-use grant consumption, response commands applied on reconnect (D-30), backfill credit.

Evidence: the retry loop or replay site, and the effect that repeats.

Claim: "When R retries or replays operation O, effect E applies twice", or "retry R has no backoff, jitter, budget or idempotency key". A key, dedup or bound on that path refutes it.

Class keys: `retry:double-apply`, `retry:no-backoff`, `retry:no-budget`, `retry:no-idempotency-key`.

Artefact: comment or table.

Closure guidance: a test that delivers the operation twice and asserts one effect, or that exhausts the budget.
