# B01 boundary-crossing-in-diff (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
In this diff, find every value that crosses a trust boundary in design/02 section 3.4 and is used on the receiving side without re-checking its shape, size or authority.

Looks in: `grdr-wire`, `grdr-hook`, `grdr-daemon`, `grdr-engine`, `grdr-uplink`, `grdr-proxy`, `grdr-mcp`.

Evidence: where the value enters (the read or decode), and its use on the receiving side with no check between.

Claim: "Value V enters across boundary X at P and is used at Q without a check of its shape, size or authority." A check on every path from P to Q refutes it.

Class keys: `unchecked-crossing:shape`, `unchecked-crossing:size`, `unchecked-crossing:authority`.

Artefact: the runner's pull-request comment.

Closure guidance: a test (a fuzz target or a negative test with the bad value) or a lint that forbids the unchecked use.
