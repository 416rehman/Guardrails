# D05 missing-deadlines (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
Find every IPC, network or subprocess call with no deadline or timeout, and every wait with no bound.

Looks in: `grdr-wire` callers, `grdr-agents` canary runs, `grdr-run`, uplink.

Evidence: the call or wait, and the absence of a deadline on it or on its callers.

Claim: "Call or wait W at P has no deadline, so a peer that never answers holds it for ever." A deadline on W or an enclosing timeout refutes it.

Class keys: `no-deadline:ipc`, `no-deadline:network`, `no-deadline:subprocess`, `no-deadline:wait`.

Artefact: list.

Closure guidance: a lint on calls without a deadline, or a test with a peer that never answers.
