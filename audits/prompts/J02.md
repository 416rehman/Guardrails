# J02 footprint-and-build-storms (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
Find idle wakeups, polling loops and timers that break the 1% idle target, resident memory above the spine's 250 MB endpoint total (section 6, excluding the on-demand Rune window) or the design/03 per-process budgets, and per-event work that skips D-52 roll-ups during a build storm.

Looks in: `grdr-daemon`, `grdr-engine`, sensors, `grdr-tray`, `grdr-desktop`.

Evidence: the timer, loop or allocation, and the budget it breaks.

Claim: "Component C wakes, polls, allocates or does per-event work at P, which breaks budget B." A measurement under the budget refutes it.

Class keys: `footprint:idle-wakeup`, `footprint:polling`, `footprint:memory`, `footprint:storm-per-event`.

Artefact: soak findings.

Closure guidance: a test: the soak and build-storm gate.
