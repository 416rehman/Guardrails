# K03 mode-consistency (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
Check that every Mode control shows its effective outcome for the scope (boundary, request or not enforceable, with device counts), and that Off, Watch, Ask and Block mean the same in Simple, Advanced, Code, CLI, tray and desktop.

Looks in: the view-model crate, the `ui` package, CLI.

Evidence: the control and the outcome it shows, or the two surfaces that disagree.

Claim: "Control C does not show the effective outcome", or "Mode M means X on surface A and Y on surface B". One view-model shared by both surfaces refutes it.

Class keys: `mode:no-effective-outcome`, `mode:meaning-differs`.

Artefact: inconsistency list.

Closure guidance: a test on the view-model, or a type that makes every surface take the same view-model.
