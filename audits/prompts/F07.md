# F07 config-reference (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
For every configuration key and `GRDR_` variable, give its default, its validation, its behaviour when invalid, and whether it is documented.

Looks in: the configuration schema, `grdr doctor` output.

Evidence: the key's declaration, its read site and its documentation, or their absence.

Claim: "Key K has no default, no validation, undefined behaviour when invalid, or no documentation." The schema entry and the documentation refute it.

Class keys: `config:no-default`, `config:no-validation`, `config:invalid-behaviour`, `config:undocumented`.

Artefact: generated reference.

Closure guidance: a lint that checks every key against the schema and the generated reference, or a test for the invalid case.
