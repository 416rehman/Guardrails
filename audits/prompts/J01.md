# J01 hot-path-cliffs (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
Find quadratic work, per-event allocation, clones, synchronous logging and repeated parsing on hot paths.

Looks in: `Engine::step`, the session monitor, `grdr-fastpath`, normalisers, uplink batching, lake flush and compaction.

Evidence: the hot-path entry, and the costly operation on it.

Claim: "Hot path H does work W (quadratic, a per-event allocation, a clone, a synchronous log or a repeated parse) at P." A bound, reuse, or a benchmark showing W constant refutes it.

Class keys: `hot-path:quadratic`, `hot-path:per-event-alloc`, `hot-path:clone`, `hot-path:sync-logging`, `hot-path:reparse`.

Artefact: benchmark additions.

Closure guidance: a test: an instruction-count gate on the path.
