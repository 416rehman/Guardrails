# I04 licence-hygiene (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
Find copyleft or source-available code, vendored copies without provenance, licence headers that disagree with SPDX, and modified copies of CC-BY-SA skills.

Looks in: `third_party/`, `bpf/` (`GPL-2.0-only OR BSD-2-Clause`), `.agents/`.

Evidence: the file, and its header, provenance file or licence record.

Claim: "File F is copyleft or source-available, lacks provenance, has a header that disagrees with SPDX, or modifies a CC-BY-SA skill." A provenance record or matching header refutes it.

Class keys: `licence:copyleft`, `licence:source-available`, `licence:no-provenance`, `licence:spdx-mismatch`, `licence:modified-cc-by-sa`.

Artefact: licence report diff.

Closure guidance: a lint on headers and provenance, or a decision record.
