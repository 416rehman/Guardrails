# D07 unbounded-resources (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
Find unbounded channels, queues, maps keyed by untrusted values, caches with no eviction, unbounded spawns, recursion, regex, decompression and body sizes. Check each against `limits.toml`.

Looks in: `limits.toml` coverage, zstd decode in uplink and server ingest, the hook payload cap of 1 MiB, the 2 MiB frame cap.

Evidence: the resource's construction or growth site, and its `limits.toml` entry or the absence of one.

Claim: "Resource R at P grows without bound under input I", or "has no entry in `limits.toml`". A bound read from `limits.toml` on that path refutes it.

Class keys: `unbounded:channel`, `unbounded:queue`, `unbounded:untrusted-key-map`, `unbounded:cache`, `unbounded:spawn`, `unbounded:recursion`, `unbounded:regex`, `unbounded:decompression`, `unbounded:body-size`.

Artefact: limits gaps.

Closure guidance: a lint (the `limits.toml` check) or a test that feeds input past the bound.
