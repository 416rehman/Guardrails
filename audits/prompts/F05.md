# F05 cedar-vocabulary-drift (version 1.0.0)

Concept:
Compare the Cedar schema, entity construction in `grdr-core::policy`, analyser vocabularies in packs and the worked policies in design/12. Flag any change that widens, including a pattern removed from a protective class; churn in restrict-only, feed-managed lists from signed intelligence packs is not a widening (D-08).

Looks in: `grdr-schema`, `grdr-core::policy`, content packs.

Evidence: the two definitions that disagree, or the widening change.

Claim: "The schema and the construction disagree on X", or "change C widens protective class K". Agreement, or a restrict-only feed list, refutes it.

Class keys: `cedar-drift:schema-mismatch`, `cedar-drift:vocabulary-mismatch`, `cedar-drift:widening`, `cedar-drift:protective-pattern-removed`.

Artefact: drift list.

Closure guidance: a test (schema against construction, and the worked policies) or a lint on widening changes.
